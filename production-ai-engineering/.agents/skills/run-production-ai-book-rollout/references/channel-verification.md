# Channel verification

Channel policies and community norms can change. Recheck official sources immediately before preparing a live channel plan and again before publishing. Record the check date and material constraints in the rollout state.

## Current routing baseline

The following was verified against official guidance on July 19, 2026:

1. **LinkedIn personal posts — primary.** Publish useful excerpts, diagrams, implementation lessons, and honest build notes with one clear paid call to action. Avoid a feed of repeated sales-only posts. Verify current [LinkedIn post capabilities](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a527227).
2. **LinkedIn newsletter — public sampling only.** Newsletters are a public LinkedIn subscription and notification layer, so use one for free field notes or shortened excerpts, not as the paid weekly product. Verify the [LinkedIn newsletter FAQ](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a517914/newsletters-on-linkedin-faq?lang=en).
3. **Hacker News — conditional.** Submit only a strong ungated sample with no signup barrier, participate personally, avoid vote solicitation, and make the technical contribution primary. Verify [Show HN rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html), [HN guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html), and any current [Show HN account restrictions](https://news.ycombinator.com/showlim).
4. **Reddit — selective and community-specific.** Participate where there is authentic fit, read each community's rules, disclose authorship, and never paste the same promotion across communities. Verify Reddit's [promotion guidance](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/28012014962580-How-do-I-keep-spam-out-of-my-community), [spam policy](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam), and [moderator-contact guidance](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043043792-How-do-I-contact-the-moderators-of-a-community).
5. **Product Hunt — exclude for the current book.** The March 2026 featuring rules excluded books, reports, and commerce sites. Reconsider only if the project becomes an independently useful software product. Verify [Product Hunt featuring guidelines](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/9883485-product-hunt-featuring-guidelines).

## Verification checklist

Before a channel-specific draft or schedule:

1. Open the official policy and current community rules.
2. Confirm the content type is allowed and the account is eligible.
3. Confirm disclosure, signup, link, solicitation, automation, and AI-content constraints.
4. Check whether an ungated sample is required or materially safer.
5. Record the source URL, check date, and constraint in `marketing/rollout-state.md`.
6. Adapt the idea to the channel; do not cross-post identical promotional copy.
7. Obtain approval for the exact final copy and external action.

If rules are ambiguous, prefer a useful ungated contribution, contact moderators when appropriate, or skip the channel.
