# Metrics and decision rules

Use outcome measures to decide whether the paid book and weekly promise create value. Treat impressions, likes, followers, and opens as diagnostics, not success.

## Funnel definitions

Define each event before collecting results:

| Metric | Definition | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualified visit | Unique person from the intended buyer group who reaches the offer | Denominator for message and channel quality |
| Checkout start | Qualified visitor who starts checkout | Detects offer interest before payment friction |
| Paid conversion | Successful first payments divided by qualified visits | Measures willingness to pay for the complete promise |
| Access success | New buyers who successfully reach the PDF within 24 hours divided by new buyers | Measures delivery reliability |
| First-update activation | New buyers who reach the first weekly update within seven days divided by eligible new buyers | Tests the recurring value loop |
| Renewal | Eligible subscriptions that renew for the next billing period divided by eligible subscriptions | Strongest early recurring-value signal |
| Voluntary churn | Customer-requested cancellations divided by subscriptions eligible to continue | Identifies value or expectation problems |
| Involuntary churn | Failed renewals not recovered divided by renewal attempts | Identifies billing-recovery problems |
| Refund rate | Refunded first payments divided by first payments | Exposes expectation or quality mismatch |
| Publishing effort | Human hours required to review, package, approve, and deliver one update | Tests operational sustainability |
| Support load | Support cases and handling time per active subscriber | Exposes delivery and clarity problems |

## Provisional founding targets

Use these only until the user approves targets in `marketing/scorecard.md`:

- Invite 20 qualified people privately.
- Seek five paid founding customers.
- Require 100% access success before expanding acquisition.
- Complete at least three useful founder conversations.
- Have the first paid weekly update ready before the first invitation.
- Resolve every critical payment, access, cancellation, or delivery failure before public launch.

These are operational learning gates, not market benchmarks and not public scarcity claims.

## Provisional first-month health targets

- At least 80% first-update activation.
- At least 70% first renewal among eligible subscribers.
- No unresolved critical access or cancellation failures.
- Median weekly production effort no more than four hours after the workflow stabilizes.

Mark these as assumptions until explicitly approved. Small cohorts produce volatile percentages; always show numerator, denominator, and the relevant customer evidence.

## Diagnose before changing

- Low qualified traffic: fix reach or channel fit; do not immediately change price.
- Visits without checkout starts: fix audience/problem/message alignment or sample quality.
- Checkout starts without payments: inspect trust, billing disclosure, technical errors, and payment friction.
- Payments without access: stop acquisition and fix delivery.
- Access without first-update activation: fix onboarding, update format, or recurring promise.
- Activation without renewal: interview customers about lasting utility and expectation mismatch.
- Healthy renewal with high publishing effort: simplify production before scaling.
- Healthy outcomes from one channel: scale only that proven channel in a bounded test.

## Decision record

For every continue, revise, pause, or scale recommendation, record:

1. Observation, with numerator, denominator, and date range.
2. Supporting customer evidence, sanitized.
3. Interpretation and competing explanation.
4. One decision.
5. One next hypothesis and its success/failure evidence.
6. Owner and review date.
7. What evidence would reverse the decision.
