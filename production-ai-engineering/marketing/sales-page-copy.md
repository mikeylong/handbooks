## A model call is one part of the product

A model can propose an answer or action. The surrounding system decides whether and how it should proceed, and remains responsible for the outcome.

The field guide connects the pieces that determine whether an AI system works in production:

- product contracts, service levels, and degraded behavior;
- harness, context, retrieval, and memory;
- inference efficiency, caches, batching, and model routing;
- structured outputs, tool execution, authorization, and termination;
- RAG, evals, observability, workflow cost, and release gates;
- safety boundaries, tenant isolation, failure diagnosis, and production readiness.

## How the Tuesday field note works

The handbook goes through a source and evidence review every Monday. Approved changes can move into the published edition after manual review.

Each Tuesday note explains what changed, why it matters, and what to do. When the edition does not change, the note works through a practical production problem using the published guide and says plainly that no new edition shipped that week.

An unapproved review candidate is never presented as current product content.
