## LinkedIn adaptation

The supplied LinkedIn example is a preview-and-unlock pattern, not a monetary paywall. LinkedIn documents the exact `Unlock full document` behavior as a Sponsored Content Document Ad with a Lead Gen Form: the advertiser chooses the preview depth, and the member submits contact information before seeing the rest of the document.

For this rollout:

1. Keep the approved Sections 1–3 sample freely available on the site without an email or payment gate.
2. For the organic personal-profile launch, create a separate two- or three-page native teaser from public material: a cover naming the problem, one useful framework or lesson, and a final route to the free sample. Put the sample link in the post copy because LinkedIn says links inside documents become clickable only after download. Anyone who can view an organic document post can download its entire file, so include no subscriber-only content.
3. If a paid test is separately approved, use the public sample—not the subscriber PDF—as the Document Ad source. Show a controlled opening preview and send the CTA to the public sample/sales path through a website-visits or website-conversions objective.
4. Do not use a Lead Gen Form as a substitute for checkout. A LinkedIn lead is not a paid subscriber and must never receive subscriber access without confirmed payment.
5. Treat the example's visible engagement as pattern evidence only, not as a launch benchmark.
