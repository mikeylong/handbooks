## Gate 0 — required before Week 1

Do not begin the calendar until all items are true:

- the `$5/month` offer shown on the sales page matches Stripe Checkout;
- test subscription, activation, PDF access, cancellation, refund, and support recovery pass;
- the complete PDF and archive have no public bypass;
- canonical analytics and attribution tests pass;
- the sample is useful, ungated, and does not expose private routes;
- the Tuesday field note and archive workflow can deliver to an approved active audience;
- the publisher name, production site, support address, privacy notice, terms, and required email footer are approved;
- an approver records a go decision for Week 1.

`Surfaces`, `https://surfaces.systems`, and `hello@surfaces.systems` remain **PROVISIONAL** until that decision is recorded.

## Paid-media gate after Week 6

A paid-test proposal can be reviewed only when all conditions are true:

- at least three verified subscriptions came from organic or individually approved outreach;
- access success is 100%;
- payment, cancellation, refund, delivery, support recovery, privacy, and attribution tests pass;
- the first Tuesday note was delivered successfully;
- the exact creative, audience, destination, budget, tracking, privacy treatment, stop rule, and approver are recorded;
- no complete-PDF material appears in the ad.

Passing the gate authorizes a decision meeting. It does not authorize spend.
