# Production AI Engineering acquisition launch kit

Status: approval-ready drafts; nothing in this directory authorizes publishing, outreach, account creation, list import, advertising, or spend  
Prepared: 2026-08-04  
Proposed execution window: 2026-08-17 through 2026-09-27, gated rather than automatic

## Decision

Start with the complete paid path, a small founding cohort, and useful LinkedIn material. Community participation comes after the public sample proves useful. Paid media stays off until organic acquisition produces at least three verified subscribers, every buyer receives working private access, and the first Tuesday field note ships successfully.

## Approved offer used in these drafts

- `$5/month`, recurring until canceled.
- Current private Production AI Engineering PDF.
- Material new PDF editions released while the subscription is active.
- One concise, source-reviewed field note every Tuesday.
- Subscriber archive while active.
- Sections 1–3 available as a useful, ungated public sample.
- Cancel anytime; access continues through the current paid period.
- First monthly charge refundable when requested within seven days; duplicate charges and payments without working access are corrected.

The terms still require the qualified legal, tax, privacy, renewal, and email-consent review already identified in the offer documents.

## Approval order

1. Confirm or replace every provisional identity and URL value.
2. Approve the exact offer and customer-facing terms against the live Checkout configuration.
3. Pass payment, entitlement, private access, cancellation, refund, support recovery, delivery, and analytics tests.
4. Approve the calendar and Week 1 recipient-selection criteria. Recipient names stay outside Git.
5. Approve one artifact at a time immediately before use.
6. Run the same-day community checklist before any Reddit, Hacker News, Slack, forum, or community action.
7. Record the actual URL, final copy digest, approver, timestamp, and result in the authorized operational system.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- The complete PDF, subscriber archive, private Blob object key, signed URL, and access token never appear in public copy or tracking.
- The free sample is useful without payment, signup, or email.
- No fabricated reader, customer, incident, performance, or adoption story appears in a post or reply.
- Mike writes community text himself when the community disallows generated or AI-edited content.
- No unsolicited bulk messages, scraped contacts, engagement coordination, copied cross-posts, link shorteners, or hidden commercial relationship.
- No paid campaign launches from this kit. The organic gate and a separate exact-budget approval still apply.
