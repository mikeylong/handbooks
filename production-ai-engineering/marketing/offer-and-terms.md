# Production AI Engineering offer and terms

Status: approved for planning; qualified review pending  
Last updated: 2026-07-20  
Target public launch: week of August 17, 2026

This document defines the working customer promise. It is product copy, not legal advice. Review applicable tax, privacy, consumer-protection, renewal, and email-consent requirements before live billing.

## Approved decisions

- Price: `$5/month`, recurring until canceled.
- Initial channel: LinkedIn.
- Paid bundle: current Production AI Engineering PDF, material new PDF editions while subscribed, and one source-reviewed field note each week.

Approval of the offer does not authorize live billing, deployment, outreach, or publication.

## Approved public sample

Keep Sections 1–3 of `AI_ENGINEERING_FIELD_GUIDE.md` public as a standalone sample:

1. The full system.
2. Harness engineering, not just prompt engineering.
3. Context engineering, not long prompts.

The sample should end with a short contents preview and one link to the `$5/month` offer. The complete handbook site and PDF become subscriber-only before promotion.

## Approved weekly promise

- Deliver one concise field note every Tuesday in Pacific time.
- Start from the Monday review and published handbook authority.
- When an approved edition change ships, explain what changed, why it matters, and what the reader should do.
- When no edition change ships, send a practical field note grounded in the published handbook and say plainly that the edition did not change that week.
- Never present an unapproved candidate as current product content.
- Keep a subscriber archive available while the subscription is active.

## Approved access and cancellation terms

- The subscription renews monthly until canceled.
- Customers may cancel at any time.
- Cancellation stops the next renewal. Access continues through the end of the current paid period.
- A downloaded PDF remains with the customer. Access to future editions and the subscriber archive ends with the paid period.
- The account and cancellation path must work without contacting support.

## Approved refund terms

- Refund the first monthly charge when requested within seven days.
- Correct duplicate charges and payments that do not produce working access.
- Handle later exceptions case by case and follow any rights required by applicable law.

The customer-facing version should be shorter than this internal specification.

## Communication terms

- The weekly field note is part of the paid service and must be disclosed at checkout.
- Service messages use `hello@surfaces.systems` as the monitored support and reply-to address.
- Promotional messages outside the paid service require the appropriate consent and unsubscribe handling.
- LinkedIn DMs are limited to individually approved founding prospects; no mass unsolicited messaging.
