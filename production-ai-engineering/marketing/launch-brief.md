## Offer

- **Buyer:** Senior/staff/principal software, platform, ML, or AI engineers; engineering managers; and technical founders responsible for production LLM systems.
- **Expensive problem:** Moving from a convincing LLM demo to a system that is reliable, observable, cost-aware, secure, and operable under real load.
- **Positioning:** A maintained field guide for the work after the demo.
- **Paid promise:** Immediate access to the current Production AI Engineering PDF, material new editions while subscribed, and one concise source-reviewed field note each week.
- **Price:** `$5/month`, approved 2026-07-19.
- **Public sample:** The title, introduction, and Sections 1–3, approved 2026-07-20; publication remains unapproved.
- **Cancellation and refunds:** Cancel anytime with access through the paid period and a seven-day first-charge refund, approved 2026-07-20; qualified review remains required before live billing.
- **Support:** `hello@surfaces.systems`, approved 2026-07-20.

## Rollout principle

Prove the complete value loop with a small paid cohort before creating public reach:

`useful sample → clear paid promise → successful access → useful first update → renewal evidence`

Likes, impressions, and email opens help diagnose that loop. They do not prove it.
