# AI Evaluation Field Guide chapter artwork

Generation mode: built-in `image_gen`, one call per chapter illustration.

Style references: selected chapter images from the Production AI Handbook at
`/Users/mike/production-ai-engineering-book/handbook/assets/chapters/`.
They were used as style references only, never as edit targets.

## Shared prompt specification

- Use case: scientific-educational or stylized-concept.
- Asset type: chapter-opening illustration for a printed A5 AI evaluation field guide.
- Style: black-and-white pointillist technical engraving with fine ink outlines,
  restrained crosshatching, and nineteenth-century scientific-instrument craft.
- Composition: one coherent mechanical or scientific metaphor, centered in a
  wide 7:4 frame, slight isometric or three-quarter view, fully visible, with
  generous white space on every side.
- Backdrop: pure untextured white with at most sparse stippled grounding directly
  beneath the apparatus.
- Constraints: no text, letters, labels, numbers, captions, borders, frames,
  color, watermark, logo, UI, dark background, decorative scenery, people, or
  photorealistic rendering.
- Output: 1659 x 948 RGB PNG original. The PDF embeds a 1200 x 686 grayscale
  derivative at 280 ppi.
