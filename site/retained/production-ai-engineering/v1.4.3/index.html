Version 1.4.3 · 2026-11-09

This edition makes 3 reader-facing changes: 2 additions, 1 correction. No guidance was removed.

Compared with Version 1.4.2.

  1. Addition · Section 17. LLM observability as a first-class discipline

    Separate monitor coverage and review delay

    Report monitor coverage, time to automated review, time to human review and escalation rate separately. An action being logged does not establish timely review, and a low flag rate does not establish good detection. State which platforms and tool paths the denominator includes.

    The existing paragraph inventories surfaces and responder availability but does not separate these operational measures.

    Sources: Anthropic Institute: Measurements for understanding the pace of AI development inside frontier labs

  2. Addition · Section 16. Evals as the release contract

    Compare safety evaluations with production decisions

    Check whether safety-evaluation behavior transfers to comparable production decisions. Match cases on the context before the decision, keep grading definitions aligned and report the remaining case-mixture differences. Where task framing may matter, compare an unchanged task with controlled descriptions of the evaluation objective. Do not assume that evaluation awareness always inflates safety scores.

    The November 3 study supports testing objective framing and the direction of transfer, absent from the current generic production-monitoring layer.

    Sources: OpenAI Alignment Research: Eval-production behavior gaps in agent safety and the role of eval-awareness

  3. Correction · Evidence-review metadata

    Record the current evidence review

    Updated the evidence-review date and limited current-access assertions to sources reopened in this prepared run.

    Readers can distinguish the common cutoff from the date on which primary evidence was checked.

    Sources: Michael Long, Surfaces: Publisher evidence-review record
