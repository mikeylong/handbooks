## Reader progress and operating boundaries

Each chapter has objectives, a worked Harborline example, an exercise with answer, a worksheet, a supported-claim limit, and sources. The worksheet index connects the twelve deliverables to the consulting packet. The capstone includes a decision worksheet, answer key and self-assessment with separate hard gates. The sequence connects discovery, opportunity choice, workflow design, governance, pilot design, delivery, system verification, adoption, evidence review, decision and handoff.

The text separates assist, recommend, decide and act. It requires runtime permissions, validated review, stop/restart ownership, recovery and evidence before wider action. Harborline and all its numbers are explicitly fictional. The consulting method, rating labels and templates are author synthesis. The source ledger separates these from external facts.

The specificity scan flagged 73 candidates. Each was reviewed in context; the flagged shapes were defined terms, scoped limitations, measures, reader tasks or comparisons supported by adjacent worked data. The dispositions and context are in `qa/specificity-review.json`.
