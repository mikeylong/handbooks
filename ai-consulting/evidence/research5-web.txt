L16: # Updated EU AI model contractual clauses
L17: ## Description
L18: 
L19: On 13 June 2024, the EU adopted the Artificial Intelligence Act (EU AI Act), the first global law regulating AI broadly. To support responsible AI procurement, the Community of Practice published EU model contractual clauses on 29 September 2023 and has now released an updated version.
L20: 
L21: In this resource, you will find the key updates:
L22:   * a full version for high-risk AI, aligned with the EU AI Act adopted on 13 June 2024;
L23:   * a light version, customisable to specific needs for non-high-risk AI;
L24:   * a commentary, providing guidance on how to use, customise and apply the clauses in practice.
