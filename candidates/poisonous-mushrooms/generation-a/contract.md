- **Title:** Poisonous Mushrooms: A Safety-First Field Handbook
- **Operating mode:** Create, first edition
- **Canonical editable source:** `source/handbook.html`
- **Previous approved PDF:** None. This is a first-edition candidate, so edition comparison is not applicable.

- **Audience:** Lay adults, parents, hikers, and beginning foragers in North America

- **Geography:** North American examples; U.S. emergency contacts are explicit. Readers elsewhere are directed to local emergency services and the WHO directory of poisons centres.
- **Governing visual system:** Bundled design system 1.0.0 and canonical specimen
- **Research cutoff and access date:** 2026-08-28
- **Authorized mutations:** Create isolated candidate source, evidence, proof, and PDF files only
- **Not authorized:** Replacing package files, publishing, committing, pushing, releasing, deploying, or contacting readers
- **Known limitations:** No local mycologist or medical toxicologist reviewed this candidate; species coverage is deliberately non-exhaustive; morphology varies with age, weather, damage, and region; symptom timing is a triage clue rather than a diagnosis.
